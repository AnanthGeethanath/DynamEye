# DynamEye
Low Vision glasses to help patients impacted with Retinal Pigmentosa (RP).

## Motivation

The [NIH](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9232096/) article, describes the desease in great detail and also clearly points to the unmet needs as on date. Quality of life of people impacted by this is drastically impacted. Approximately 1 person in every 4,000 people are impacted. This project aims to develop a highly innovative solution leveraging *Artificial Intelligence* to change the lives of millions of people. One of the key sucess factors for this to enable **everyone** by making it highly affordable - a farmer in rural areas of India needs to afford this solution.
